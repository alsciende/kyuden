# Web client (`front/`)

The site of the players, a single-page application (Vue 3, vue-router, Vite, TypeScript) that only talks to the HTTP API, on the same origin. In development, the `node` service runs the Vite server and FrankenPHP (the `symfony` service, `docker/dev/symfony/Caddyfile`) sends it every path but `/api` and `/bundles`, which go to Symfony, and the Mercure hub; in production, `npm run build` gives static files (`front/dist`) for FrankenPHP, every unknown path falling back to `index.html`. Desktop and tablet: designed for 1536px, at least 1133px wide (an iPad mini in landscape: `min-width` of the body); the screens of a game take the visible height (`100dvh`). A phone (the short side of its screen under 600px) gets the layout of a tablet, scaled down by the browser (`lib/phoneViewport.ts`, before the application is mounted): in landscape, the viewport is as wide as the board needs to be as tall as on an iPad mini (744px: 1613px on an iPhone of 852 × 393), without an initial scale, so that the browser fits it to the room it really has (the safe area of a notch); held upright, a notice over every page asks to turn it (`components/PortraitNotice.vue`, `data-phone` on the root). Touch screens get the same screens: what a tooltip tells is also written where a click leads (the effect of a ring in its window, under the ring chosen and under each ring to switch with), a press held half a second on an element with a tooltip shows it above the finger until the next touch, without clicking the element (`lib/tooltip.ts`; no callout nor selection there, `.has-tip`), the hover styles apply only where the device can hover (`@media (hover: hover)`), and the labels of the icons of a card opened are written under them on a touch screen only (`@media (hover: none)`). Installable (a PWA, `vite-plugin-pwa` in `vite.config.ts`, the build only): the manifest (`manifest.webmanifest`, the icons of `public/icons`, from the ensō of the logo) and a service worker (`sw.js`, Workbox) that precaches the shell of the client (`index.html`, the scripts, the styles) and opens the routes of the client from it, never what Symfony and the hub serve (`/api`, `/.well-known`, `/bundles`); a new release replaces it at once and reloads the open pages (`registerType: 'autoUpdate'`), the game being read again from the API.

- **Home of an account** (`/`, `views/StartView.vue`: an account gets `views/HomeView.vue`, anyone else the way of the newcomers): play a game (the queue, where the house bot is faced too, or a friendly game) or watch one (the games in progress, the first two shown, read again like those of `/watch`). While the account waits in the queue, the tile "Play" tells the wait, on a dark ground (`components/queue/QueueWaiting.vue`); otherwise how many players wait in the queue (`components/queue/QueueCount.vue`, read again every 15 seconds while the page is visible: `lib/useQueueCount.ts`; mockup `XQrxVW5j55szWxXpWXWPPt`). Signing in leads there.
- **Waiting in the queue** (`lib/queueing.ts`; mockup `KJWPhVGdXGkiftvaAB5XFH`): the wait is shared by every page and kept by the tab (`sessionStorage`: the deck, whom the user waits for, since when), the queue (`GET /api/queue`) telling whether it goes on. Once in the queue, an account goes home and may watch a game or a replay meanwhile; a guest, who cannot watch, waits on the screen of the queue (dark, `.inverted`). `components/QueueFollower.vue`, mounted by `App.vue`, reads the queue when the session changes, follows the topic of the user on the hub while they wait (`game_started`) and reads the queue again every 15 seconds (`lib/visiblePolling.ts`: a notification may be lost; no longer in the queue, their last game in progress is the one found, else they left it elsewhere); once found, the game is read and the match is announced over the page for 2.5 seconds, like a fight about to begin (`components/MatchAnnouncement.vue`, logic in `lib/matchAnnouncement.ts`: the nobori of the user sweeping in from the left, that of the opponent from the right, a vermilion slash and 対 between them, "Opponent found · Your game opens"; at once without motion), or, the game not read within 1.5 seconds, "Opponent found" for a second; then the game opens under it (a game watched is left: its spectator goes), which fades out once the table is drawn (`tableSet`, at most 4 seconds), so that nothing shows between them. A game against the house bot is announced the same way (`gameFound()` from the queue). The wait shows under the header of the other pages and at the top of the bar on the right of a game watched or replayed (`components/queue/QueueBanner.vue`), each with the button that leaves the queue (`lib/useLeaveQueue.ts`: the host, whose invitation it cancels, goes back to `/invite`).
- **Watching a game** (`/watch`, `views/WatchView.vue`, accounts only; logic in `lib/watch.ts`): the games in progress (`GET /api/games`, `lib/useGamesInProgress.ts`: read again every 15 seconds while the page is visible, at once when it is visible again, `lib/visiblePolling.ts`): the players on the tints of their clans (`components/MatchUp.vue`), the tags Bot, Friendly and Your game, the phase and the step, the number of spectators; "Watch" opens the game, "Join" opens a game of the user as its player.
- **Newcomers**: the way to a first game, for a player who knows the game but not the site: the clan (`/`, the seven Great Clans with their starter deck, stronghold and champion read from `GET /api/cards/{code}`), the name at court (`/name`, drawn from the families of the clan, `lib/names.ts`), the opponent (`/opponent`: `POST /api/guests`, then `POST /api/bot-games`), then the game (`/games/:id`). The choices are kept by the tab (`lib/newcomer.ts`, `sessionStorage`), the guest account by the browser (`lib/session.ts`, `localStorage`: the same name keeps the same guest; an invalid token makes a new one). An account plays the introductory game under its own name.
- **Accounts** (`lib/session.ts`, `lib/credentials.ts`: the rules of the username and the password, checked before sending; `components/PasswordField.vue`: the field of a password, with a button to read what was typed, hidden again once the form is sent): sign in (`/login`, from the header: `POST /api/sessions`), create an account (`/signup`: the preferred clan first (`components/ClanPicker.vue`), prefilled for a guest with the clan of their last game, else of their way as a newcomer, else drawn at random (`suggestedClan()`), then a username and a password; a guest keeps their user, their name at court and their games; anyone else gets a name at court drawn by the client from the families of the clan; offered to a guest at the end of a game), the account (`/account`: the username, the preferred clan, the name at court, the nobori (its mon and its pattern among those earned, every other one shown dimmed with a padlock, "Not earned yet" on a click; saved at once, `lib/nobori.ts`) and how the board draws the cards (drawn or their images) and the appearance (day, night or as the system) changed with `PATCH /api/self`, the linked accounts (link a provider, unlink it once confirmed), sign out with `DELETE /api/sessions/current`, delete the account once confirmed in its row (`DELETE /api/self`), and at its foot "Your games" and "Your trophies", then the landing page); signing in with a provider (`lib/oauth.ts`: the buttons of `/login` and `/account` for the providers of `GET /api/oauth/providers`; the flow started, its state kept by the tab (`sessionStorage`), the provider sends the visitor back to `/oauth/:provider/callback` (`views/OAuthCallbackView.vue`), which refuses a state the tab did not start, completes the flow, then leads to the account or back with what went wrong (`?oauth=not_linked | linked_elsewhere | failed`); an identity linked to another account offers to sign in to that one instead), the queue (`/queue`: the user chooses one of their decks or a starter deck (each with the mon of the clan of its stronghold and the name of the stronghold, as the API tells them; the deck of `?deck=` chosen, when it is one of them, from the page of a deck), or imports a deck of EmeraldDB by pasting the URL of its page (`POST /api/decks` with `url`; the deck imported comes first and is chosen, the reasons of a refusal from `lib/queue.ts`: the cards unknown to the engine, those of Emerald Legacy, or the rules broken), then joins the queue (see "Waiting in the queue"; how many players wait is told above the button, `QueueCount`, not for a friendly game); the same with an invitation), or faces the house bot with it ("Face Kyūden no Karō": `POST /api/bot-games`, `data/houseBot.ts`, the game opens at once); A deck of their own shows a button to delete it, confirmed in its tile; the deck chosen deleted, nothing is chosen. A deck imported that they already had is chosen in its place ("You already have …"). The session (`session`, a ref) knows whether it is a guest (no username); the header shows the name of an account, else the way to sign in. A token of the session the API refuses (a 401: revoked, or forgotten by a database created again) is forgotten (`followRefusedTokens()`, told by `request()` of `api/client.ts`): a guest comes back as a newcomer, an account signs in again (`/login?next=`, back to the page it was on).
- **Decks** (`/decks` and `/decks/:id`, `views/DecksView.vue` and `views/DeckView.vue`, accounts only; logic in `lib/decks.ts`; mockup `Dh5wCnuitjGT1eQbseC5cC`): linked from the header, beside the account ("Decks", marked while in the section). The list: the decks of the user (`GET /api/self/decks`), the last played first, each with the mon of its clan, its name, its stronghold, the games played and won, the win rate and the day of its last game; below it, a deck of EmeraldDB imported from the URL of its page (`POST /api/decks`, the refusals told as in the queue, `importProblem()`), the deck imported first in the list, marked "New", or "You already have …". The page of a deck (`GET /api/self/decks/{id}`): the mon, the clan, the stronghold and the role, its name and how many cards of each clan it has (the most first, the neutral cards last, `clanCounts()`); exported in the format of Jigoku, which EmeraldDB imports ("Export", before "Rename": a line `<count> <title> (<pack>)` per card, in the order of the page, nothing else, `jigokuExport()`; copied to the clipboard, "Copied, ready to paste", or shown in a field to select when the browser refuses to copy it), renamed in place (the name becomes a field: Enter saves, Escape cancels; `PATCH /api/decks/{id}`) or deleted once confirmed in place (back to the list; a refusal of the API told there); "Play with it" leads to the queue with the deck chosen (`/queue?deck=`). Its record: five tiles (played, and in progress; won and lost, with their share; ranked, the standard games; the day of the last game and its result against the clan faced), then how the games ended and against each clan, won and lost. Its cards by their titles, read from `GET /api/cards` once per page (`fetchCards()`, cacheable: the browser keeps it): the image of the stronghold beside the stronghold and the role, the provinces with the icons of their elements (the font of fiveringsdb, the name of the element in their tooltip), then two columns, the dynasty deck (characters, holdings, events) and the conflict deck (characters, attachments, events), each card with its quantity, in the order of the titles; the image of a card shows beside it under the pointer (`lib/preview.ts`, `CardPreview.vue`, as on the board).
- **Friendly games** (`lib/invitation.ts`): an account invites its bot or a friend (`/invite`, from "Play a friendly game" on the account and the queue): for the bot, the configuration of an MCP client with the key (kept by the tab to show it again), a prompt of one line and the key alone; for a friend, the link `/join/<code>`. Then the host chooses their deck in the queue with the invitation (`/queue?invitation=`): "You face <bot>, and nobody else", then the wait at home like any other ("Waiting for your bot"), with the time the invitation still waits; leaving cancels it. The link (`views/JoinView.vue`) tells who invites; a visitor with a session goes to the queue with the code (`/queue?code=`, open to a guest), anyone else chooses a clan and a name at court, becomes a guest and enters the queue with its starter deck ("I have an account": `/login?next=`); an invitation that can no longer be used says so.
- **Tournaments** (`lib/tournaments.ts`: the dates in the time zone of the viewer, named, the list split by where each stands, the size of the bracket, the starts offered; `lib/tournamentEntries.ts`: the registrations of the account, shared by the pages; mockup `ShNGfp7Ma6k8tazb1dBaVD`): public, visitors included. A player registered in a cancelled tournament sees an alert under the header on every page but those of a game (`components/tournaments/CancelledTournamentAlert.vue`, read again on every page), with a link to it and "Unregister", until they unregister.
