# Game flow

How the engine implements the rules of a round (`doc/timing-and-gameplay.md`), as a board: what it enforces, and the effect messages that let the players apply the cards.

- **Values from players**: since the engine does not apply card effects, a step whose outcome cards can modify (e.g. the amount of fate collected) is a player action carrying the value; an `auto` transition guarded on every player having acted then moves the game on.
- **Turn records**: what a player did during the round goes in the turn records of `PlayerState`, reset at the end of the round (`fate_phase_ended`).
- **Player order**: stored in `GameState` (`playerIds`, fixed by `start_game` from the order of arrival, `Player::joinRank`; the first player changes, not the order).
- **Opportunities to act**: the players who have the opportunity to act are the active players of `GameState` (`activePlayerIds`); the handler of each action rejects a player who is not active (`NotActivePlayerException`). The enter listener of the step activates them (`FirstPlayerOpportunityEnterListener` and `AllPlayersOpportunityEnterListener` cover the common cases):
  - in steps resolved in player order, only the first player: the handler gives the opportunity to act to the next player (`GameState::getNextPlayerId()` / `getPlayerIdsAfter()`, resolved with `Game::getPlayer()`). An action that requires follow-up actions keeps the opportunity with the player, who ends it with `EndResolution` (see below);
  - in steps where players act simultaneously (e.g. `reveal_honor_dials`, the `react_to_*` reaction windows of a conflict), every player: they keep their opportunity until they pass.

  A player who passes (`PassOpportunity`, in a place marked `opportunities`; `PlayerState::passed`, reset by the enter listener of the step) is no longer active and is skipped by `Service/OpportunityService`. In an action window (`PassingRule::Consecutive`), a player who ends their action (`EndResolution`) resets every pass, so the players who had passed get opportunities to act again.
- **Resolving what a player plays**: an active player who has done nothing passes (`PassOpportunity`); once they act (`PlayActionEvent`, `TriggerActionAbility`, `PlayCharacter`, `PlayAttachment`, `PlayCharacterFromProvince`: `OpportunityService::takeAction()`, `PlayerState::acted`), they apply its effects with other messages, then end it (`EndResolution`), which ends their opportunity: they cannot pass meanwhile (`ResolutionInProgressException`), and `EndResolution` is rejected when there is nothing to end (`NothingToResolveException`). An interrupt or a reaction (`PlayInterruptEvent`, `PlayReactionEvent`, `TriggerInterruptAbility`, `TriggerReactionAbility`) is resolved the same way by any player, without using an opportunity to act, and `EndResolution` ends the last one not ended first, during a duel too; the action itself ends once no duel is being resolved. The events played and the abilities triggered (not the characters and attachments played) are recorded in `PlayerState::resolutions` (`State/Model/Resolution`: the card whose text it is, an attachment for the abilities it grants, and the timing), in the order they were initiated: an interrupt or a reaction leaves it when it ends, the events and abilities of the action with it; the interrupts and reactions not ended yet are counted from it. `available_actions` therefore never lists both `pass_opportunity` and `end_resolution`, and the view of each player shows `acted`, `resolving_interrupts_and_reactions` and `resolutions` (`View/Model/ResolutionView`: the card as the viewer sees it now, by `Visibility`, nothing once it no longer exists, and the timing), so that the players see which effect is being applied. Phase actions that move the game on (declaring a conflict, defenders, a bid…) and `InitiateDuel` (an effect of an ability) are not actions to end.
- **End of the game**: `Service/VictoryService` checks the victory conditions from the state (a player with 25 or more honor wins; a player who has conceded (`ConcedeGame`, `PlayerState::conceded`) or has no honor is eliminated and the last one standing wins; ties go to the first player, then in player order). The guard of `end_game` uses it, and its transition listener records `winnerId` and `victoryCondition` in `GameState`. Honor cannot go below 0 (`PlayerState::setHonor()`).
- **Abandoned games** (`Service/Abandonment`): the player left alone ends the game, which they win (`ClaimAbandonment`, a platform message: the players it waited for are eliminated, `PlayerState::abandoned`, victory condition `abandonment`, `GameAbandonedEvent`). The game waits for the players to whom an action moving it on is available (`AwaitedPlayersResolver`, asking the handlers: passing, ending the resolution, bidding, declaring a conflict, defenders, a total, a dueling statistic, ending a duel…), the defending player while the attacked province is face down, the players with a face-down dynasty card while they are revealed, the player who would pass or end what they resolve but for a province the conflict is moved to face down; while players have forced reactions to trigger, those players only; the effects on the board, available to everybody, move nothing on. A player may end it once it has waited for their opponents only (not for them) for 3 minutes without changing (`AbandonmentClaimer::INACTIVITY`), or for 1 minute once the opponent no longer reads it (`PlayerPresence`: not seen for `ABSENT_AFTER`, 60 seconds; every read of a player through the HTTP API or the MCP tools writes `Player::lastSeenAt`, at most every 10 seconds, in a statement of its own). The time is checked by `AbandonmentClaimer` before sending the message, with the version it checked (`ExpectedVersionStamp`, the entity manager cleared so that the lock reads the game again); the handler only checks the state, so that the records replay. Nothing else ends a game while a player still reads it: the player may wait longer. A game every player has left, unchanged and read by none of them for an hour (`AbandonedGamesEnder::LEFT_AFTER`), is ended by the platform (`EndAbandonedGame`, a platform message: every player eliminated, nobody wins: `winnerId` null, victory condition `abandonment`, `GameEndedWithoutWinnerEvent`), so that both players gone or the human gone in a game of the house bot (which never reads the game nor claims) do not stay in progress: the task `Task/EndAbandonedGames` of the worker, every 5 minutes (`App\Schedule`), finds them in one query (`GameRepository::findLeftSince()`) and sends the message on each with the version it read.
- **Reaction windows** (`doc/card-scripts.md`, "Reaction offers"): once a triggering condition has occurred, the engine offers each player the cards they can react with, in a window of their state (`PlayerState::reactionWindows`), each with the id shared by the players offered something, the trigger and the cards (`ability_windows` in the view, of timing `reaction`, the newest first since nested sequences complete last in, first out; of the viewer only: what the opponent can react with is theirs). It blocks nothing and changes no opportunity: the reactions are still accepted anywhere, the player answers or not. A card leaves the newest window offering it once its player triggers its reaction or plays it as a reaction (`OpportunityService::startInterruptOrReaction()`); every window of a player closes when they pass and at the end of the round. Declining is not a message, so that the opponent does not learn (the log, the version, the notification) that the player had a card to react with: the web client forgets the cards declined. The limits of the cards are not checked. A forced reaction ("Forced Reaction:") is offered marked forced (`forced_card_ids` of the window) and cannot be declined; the view of each player tells every reader the forced reactions that player has to trigger (`forced_reactions`: `Service/CardScript/ForcedReactionsResolver`, those of their windows they can trigger now, `AbilityService::canTrigger()`, so that a card gone elsewhere blocks nobody), the cards being visible to every player. The engine enforces nothing: the web client moves the game on for neither player while one is pending.
- **Interrupt windows** (`interrupts.md`): a message or an automatic transition (metadata `interrupt`) that would make a triggering condition occur (a card would leave play: `Trigger/CardLeavingPlayTrigger`; the effects of an event or an ability would initiate: `Trigger/EffectsInitiatingTrigger`; an attachment would be played: `Trigger/AttachmentBeingPlayedTrigger`; a province would be broken: `Trigger/ProvinceBreakingTrigger`) waits in an interrupt window while a player is asked (`Service/Interrupt/InterruptOfferer`, on what every player sees: a card in sight that can interrupt it, or an event out of sight whose copy is in the hand or the conflict deck), then is done once the window closes (`InterruptWindowMiddleware`), unless cancelled (`CancelImminentTrigger`, special: the replacement itself applied with the effect messages); an event or an ability cancelled is still played, its resolution marked cancelled (`Resolution::$cancelled`), which its player only ends. The windows are a stack (`GameState::interruptWindows`, nested sequences), each with its stages (would, forced, standard); the players asked answer at once (`PassInterrupt`, an interrupt initiated cancelling the passes), and the window waits for the interrupts initiated in it to end. While one is open, the engine refuses what does not answer it (`InterruptWindowOpenException`), the automatic transitions but the end of the game wait, the provinces are not refilled; `ability_windows` shows it to every player (the cards to the viewer only), first.
- **Duels**: a duel is not a place of the `game` workflow, since it returns to the action window it was initiated in: it is the `DuelState` of `GameState`, whose steps the messages move on (`InitiateDuel`, `BidHonor`, `DeclareDuelStatistic`, `EndDuel`). `CancelDuel` (special: Stay Your Hand) ends it at any step without result, the honor dials not revealed forgotten: an effect accepted in any place, from any player, while a duel is being resolved (`DuelCancelledEvent`). While a duel is being resolved, no action window is open and the players cannot pass or end their action (`OpportunityService::assertNoDuel()`); interrupts and reactions can be ended.
- **Interrupts and reactions** are initiated when their triggering condition occurs, not during an opportunity to act: `PlayInterruptEvent`, `PlayReactionEvent`, `TriggerInterruptAbility` and `TriggerReactionAbility` are accepted from any player in any place, during a duel too, and change no opportunity: the player ends them with `EndResolution`. Action events and abilities (`PlayActionEvent`, `TriggerActionAbility`) require the opportunity to act in an action window. An event is played with the message of its timing (`AbilityTiming`), whatever the hand holds (an event may be played from elsewhere, and the decks are secret); the abilities of cards are not checked against their timings, since cards gain and lose abilities.
- **Secret information**: what a player knows and their opponents do not is stored apart from what is public, so that its visibility only depends on the state (e.g. `PlayerState::pendingHonorBid`, the secret bid, and `honorBid`, the revealed one).
- **Mulligan** (glossary, Mulligan; setup 6 and 7): once the provinces are filled (`setup_game`), the game waits in `dynasty_mulligan`, then, once the starting hands are drawn (`dynasty_mulligan_ended`), in `conflict_mulligan`. In each, the players mulligan in player order, from the first player (`MulliganEnterListener`, the handler giving the opportunity to the next player; `MulliganEndedGuardListener` lets the step end once nobody is active): `MulliganDynastyCards` (face-down dynasty cards of their regular provinces) and `MulliganConflictCards` (cards of their hand), with the cards to set aside, none to keep them (`MulliganDeclinedEvent`, "{player} keeps their cards"). As many cards are put into the provinces or drawn from the top of the deck, then the cards chosen are shuffled back into it (`Service/MulliganService`, `CardsMulliganedEvent`). The face-down dynasty cards are hidden from their owner too (the visibility only depends on the state): entering the dynasty mulligan, each player looks at the cards of their provinces (`CardInspector`, in the log for them only), and again once they have mulliganed, so that the client shows them while the step lasts. A game has a mulligan when `CreateGame` says so (`mulligan`, `GameState::mulligan`): the queue and the invitations (`AutomaticGameLauncher`) and `app:game:create`, not the games of the house bot, whose introductory game goes straight to the board, nor the games created before (false by default, so that their records replay the same); without it, both places are passed at once.
- **Refilling the provinces**: once a dynasty card has left a province (played from it, discarded) and no dynasty card remains on it (a holding keeps it from being refilled), the engine refills it, face down, from the dynasty deck of its owner, once the effect is resolved: "after all reaction opportunities to that card leaving the province are passed" (glossary, Refill a Province). The handlers moving dynasty cards out of provinces schedule the province (`Service/ProvinceRefiller::schedule()`, `GameState::provincesToRefill`), and `ProvinceRefillMiddleware` refills the scheduled provinces still empty after each message, once nobody resolves what they have played (an action not ended, an interrupt or a reaction) and no duel is being resolved: a character played from a province is replaced once its player ends the action. The framework steps refill at once (the broken provinces of the fate phase). `FillProvince` remains for setup and the effects that fill a province or add a card to it: the top card of the dynasty deck of its owner onto a regular province, face down or face up (`face_up`: Rally, refilling face up), whatever lies on it already (City of the Rich Frog); a province filled by an effect before the engine refills it is not refilled.
- **Playing from elsewhere than the hand** (Kunshu, Artisan Academy, Prepared Ambush, Gateway to Meido…): `PlayCharacter`, `PlayAttachment` and the events take a card of any player from where a card of its class is played (`Service/PlayService`): a conflict card of a hand, a conflict deck (any card: one looked at before) or a conflict discard pile; a dynasty character face up in a province, of a dynasty deck or a dynasty discard pile; a card set aside (removed from the game, under a card: Stolen Secrets, Back-Alley Hideaway). The effect allowing it is not checked; the opportunity, the action window and the resolution are those of a card in hand. The sender controls what they play (`take_control` once in play when they do not own it: both are logged, the play as `{player} plays {card}…`, the player of the context of the transition), an event goes to the discard pile of its owner (`play_event`), and a province a character leaves is refilled once the action is ended. A dynasty event is played from its province face up, during the dynasty phase only (glossary, Event Cards: `NotInDynastyPhaseException` otherwise), with the message of its timing like any event; it goes to the dynasty discard pile of its owner, the province being refilled once it is resolved. `PlayCharacterFromProvince` remains the play of the dynasty phase.
- **Costs and effects**: board manipulations (`BowCard`, `LoseHonor`, `GainFate` / `LoseFate`, `DiscardCard`, `HonorCharacter`, `MoveToConflict`…) are accepted in any place, from any player of the game, on any card or player, since the engine does not know which ability is being resolved.
- **Switching the conflict type** (`SwitchConflictType`): an effect accepted in any place during a conflict, from any player; the type toggles.
- **Conflict opportunities by an effect**: `GrantConflictOpportunity` (`target_player_id`, `type`) gives a player an additional conflict of a type this round: one more conflict opportunity (`PlayerState::conflictOpportunities`) and one more conflict of that type allowed (`additionalConflictTypes`, beside `declaredConflictTypes`; `PlayerState::canDeclareConflict()`, and `declarable_conflict_types` in the view of the player), until the end of the round; the conflict phase goes on while a player has an opportunity left. `DeclareNextConflict` (`target_player_id`, a player with an opportunity left) makes the target declare the next conflict out of order, with one of their own opportunities (`GameState::nextConflictPlayerId`, read and cleared by `DeclareConflictEnterListener` before the order, which goes on after them; cleared when the phase begins). Accepted in any place, from any player, for any player; the limits of the cards ("Max 1 per phase") are the players' business.
- **Action opportunities by an effect** (`GiveActionOpportunity`: `target_player_id`, `actions`, 1 by default; Child of the Plains, Hawk Tattoo, Current of the Beryt, Austere Exemplar): the next opportunities to act given in an action window go to the target, whoever would get them otherwise (`PlayerState::givenActions`, one less each time, `given_actions` in the view): at the end of an action, after a pass, or when an action window opens (`GivenActionOpportunityEnterListener`, after the listener activating the first player or the defender), through `OpportunityService`; not outside of an action window. The actions add up for the same player and replace those given to another player; a player who passes in an action window loses those left. Accepted in any place, from any player, for any player.
- **Rings**: the place of a ring is the contested ring, the unclaimed ring pool or the claimed ring pool of a player, where it keeps the type of the conflict it was claimed in (`RingState::claimedConflictType`, which card effects refer to). `SwitchRing` switches the places of two rings, in any place, from any player: a ring going to a claimed ring pool takes the type of the ring it replaces, the conflict keeps its type, and the contested ring is only switched with a ring of the pools of the attacking player, who takes the fate on the new contested ring. `ClaimRing` makes a player claim a ring they have not claimed: nobody's, in the type of the conflict being resolved or the type given (the winner of the conflict does not claim a contested ring claimed this way), or another player's, taken from them in the type it was claimed in unless another is given (`RingTakenEvent`). `ReturnRing` returns a claimed ring to the unclaimed ring pool with its fate (not the contested ring: a conflict always has a ring). `PlaceFateOnRing` and `RemoveFateFromRing` place fate from the general token pool on any ring, or return it there.
- **Looking at cards and revealing them** (`LookAtCards`, `RevealCards`: the top cards of a deck, a hand (or cards of it drawn at random by the generator of the game, `random`: Court of Justice, Kitsuki Chiari) or the dynasty cards in the provinces (`Enum/HiddenPile`, an entry of the log per province), of any player; `LookAtCard`, `RevealCard`: a card; `SearchConflictDeck`, `SearchDynastyDeck`: the whole deck of a player, special, so that the scripts of the cards that "search your deck" name them (glossary, Search), the deck then shuffled with its own message; "search the top N cards" is `LookAtCards`; their log tells the search only, "{player} searches {stack}", the cards being in its params): nothing changes in the state; the log gives the cards to the players who learn them (`Service/CardInspector`: the sender and those who already see them for a look, every player for a reveal), the others read how many cards and where. The client of the sender keeps them in sight until the player is done with them.
- **Hands and decks** (`AddCardToHand`: a conflict card of a deck or a conflict discard pile; `PutCardOnDeck`: on top or at the bottom; `ShuffleIntoDeck`; `DiscardCard`, from a deck too): effects accepted in any place, from any player, on any card; a card leaving play or a province follows the same rules as a discard.
- **Putting cards into play** (`PutCharacterIntoPlay`: a character of a hand, face up in a province, of a deck (a card looked at), of a discard pile or set aside, at home or into the conflict on the side of its controller, its owner or the player given (`controller_id`); `PutAttachmentIntoPlay`: an attachment of a hand, a conflict deck, a conflict discard pile or set aside, onto a character in play or a province (`target_card_id`), under the control of its owner or the player given; `PutHoldingIntoProvince`: a holding of the dynasty deck, the dynasty discard pile or set aside into a regular province of its owner): effects accepted in any place, from any player, on any card, without cost nor opportunity to act; a province a character leaves is refilled, a province a holding enters keeps its cards and is no longer refilled while the holding remains (Rebuild: the card shuffled out of the province is not replaced, the holding being there once the effect is resolved).
- **Moving fate** (`MoveFate`): from a holder to another, a fate pool, a character in play or a ring (`Enum/FateHolder`); the fate from or to the general token pool is gained, lost, placed or removed with the other messages (`PlaceFateOnRing` and `RemoveFateFromRing` for a ring).
- **Revealing the attacked province**: once a conflict is declared, the game waits in `reveal_attacked_province` (the defending player active) until the attacked province is faceup, before the reactions to the declaration; a province already faceup passes the step at once. The defending player reveals it with `RevealProvince`, which turns any facedown province face up, of any player (card effects reveal provinces), in any place. `TurnProvinceFacedown` (special) turns an unbroken face-up province face down, with its cards and its tokens, keeping its id (Kitsuki Yaruma, Untamed Steppe…); the Eminent keyword is not checked.
- **Moving the conflict** (`MoveConflict`, `AddAttackedProvince`, special; `Service/AttackedProvinceService`): during a conflict, in any place, from any player, the conflict moves with its contested ring to another province of the defending player (Chasing the Sun, Golden Plains…: it replaces the province the ring leaves in `ConflictState::attackedProvinceIds` and becomes `provinceId`), or is also at it (A War on Two Fronts: added to the attacked provinces, each broken separately). The province is unbroken and not attacked yet; the stronghold restriction is not checked (the effect decides). A facedown province stays so until a player reveals it with `RevealProvince` (glossary, Facedown Province: "immediately turn the province faceup"), so that the players see it revealed and think of their reactions: meanwhile, outside the step `reveal_attacked_province`, nobody passes (`PassOpportunity`) nor ends what they resolve (`EndResolution`): `AttackedProvinceService::assertRevealed()`, checked last by their handlers, so that `AwaitedPlayersResolver` waits for the player it blocks; the house bot reveals its own province, the web client offers the revelation in the panel of actions (`provinceToReveal()`). Once the strengths are recorded (`determine_result`), the new province gets its printed strength (`ConflictService::printedStrength()`), which the defending player may declare; the strength of a province left is forgotten. `DeclareConflict` attacks the stronghold province before 3 other provinces are broken with `ignore_stronghold_restriction` (Scouted Terrain, not checked).
- **Restoring a province** (`RestoreProvince`, special: Ethereal Alignment and Akodo Toshiro, a province not broken after all; Public Forum cancels its breaking in its interrupt window): a broken province of any player is face up and unbroken again (transition `restore`), with what lies on it; in any place, from any player. A broken stronghold province has ended the game.
- **Redistributing the cards of the provinces** (`RedistributeProvinceCards`, special: `target_player_id`, `card_ids` and the regular province of each, `province_ids`; Governor's Spy, once `LookAtCards` on the provinces): the face-up cards are turned face down where they are (`turn_facedown`, logged: a holding returns its honor tokens, the cards under it are discarded), then every card is deleted and created again face down in its province, with a new id drawn from the generator of the game (as a shuffle, `StackShuffler`), without a transition, so that the log tells the other players that the provinces were redistributed (`ProvinceCardsRedistributedEvent`), not where each card went; a province left without a dynasty card is refilled once the effect is resolved. The stronghold province takes no part; "each province ends up with at least one card" is not checked.
- **Control** (`TakeControl`, glossary "Control and Ownership"): the controller of a card is its owner unless another player has taken control of it in play (`CardState::controllerId`, read through `Service/ControlService`). The sender takes control of a character or an attachment in play, in any place, of any player (its owner taking it back the same way: the engine does not end effects such as "until the end of the conflict"); the transition `take_control` logs it ("{controller} takes control of {card}"). A character in play is always the first card of a stack of its controller (`Player::getCharactersInPlay()` reads the stacks): it moves there with its attachments, which keep their controller, keeps its state and participates on the side of its new controller. The rules read the controller: participating, defending, being evaded, duels, abilities, moving to the conflict, the honor of a character leaving play. Leaving play ends the control (its `CardState` is reset): the card goes to an out-of-play area of its owner.
- **Characters as attachments** (`PlayCharacterAsAttachment`, special: Tattooed Wanderer, Togashi Kazue, Serene Ise Zumi, Voice of the Ancestors…): a character played as an attachment, like `PlayAttachment` (its action window, cost and resolution) from where a character is played (`PlayService`), onto a character in play of any player (not a province), under the control of the sender (transition `play_as_attachment`, to the place `attached`). It is an attachment then: it does not participate, its printed skills modify nothing (glossary, Attachment Cards), it bows, readies, triggers its abilities, changes control and moves (`MoveAttachment`, to a character only) like one, and is discarded when its character leaves play. `DetachAsCharacter` (special: Promising Youth, Togashi Hoshi), in any place, from any player, turns a card attached to a character (a character played as an attachment, or an attachment) into a character in play, ready, at home, in a new stack of its controller (transition `detach`); an attachment turned into a character has its printed modifiers as skills (0 without one), as the web client and the house bot read them.
- **Other effects on the board** (`MoveAttachment`: an attachment in play (or a character played as one) to another character in play or a province, keeping its owner; `SwitchProvinceCards`: two dynasty cards of different provinces of the same player, each keeping its orientation; `PutCardIntoProvince`: a dynasty card of a dynasty deck (known through `LookAtCards`), a dynasty discard pile, a province or in play (it leaves play) into another province of its owner, the stronghold province included, face up or face down (`face_up`), beside the cards there, the province it leaves being refilled (transitions `move_to_province_faceup` and `move_to_province_facedown`); `BreakProvince`: a face-up province outside of the resolution of a conflict, a broken stronghold province ending the game; `TakeFirstPlayerToken`: the first player changes, not the player order; `DiscardImperialFavor`: the Imperial Favor returns to its unclaimed state, refused when nobody has it; `TakeImperialFavor`: a player claims it, from its unclaimed state or from a player, on the side given, without moving the game on, unlike `ClaimImperialFavor` after the glory count): accepted in any place, from any player, on any card or player.
- **Setting cards aside** (out of play): `RemoveFromGame` (`face_down`: Stolen Secrets) puts a card into the cards its owner has removed from the game (Shadow Step), or, face down, into those of the sender, who alone knows it then (the card stolen is not shown to its owner); `PlaceCardUnder` (`host_card_id`, `face_down`) puts a card under a card in play (a character, an attachment), a dynasty card face up in a province (Back-Alley Hideaway), a province (Kaiu Shihobu) or a stronghold (Under Siege), the owner of that card knowing it when it is face down (Daidoji Uji). Both take a card of a deck, a hand, a province, play or a discard pile, or a card already set aside (transitions `remove_from_game` and `place_under`, with their `_facedown` variants, `WorkflowConfig::setAside()`); a card leaving play follows the rules of a discard, a province it leaves is refilled. From there, a card is played (`Service/PlayService`), put into play, into a province (`PutCardIntoProvince`, `PutHoldingIntoProvince`), into a hand (`AddCardToHand`), on a deck or into the discard pile. The cards under a card in play or in a province are discarded once it leaves its place (`Workflow/HostLeaveListener`; a bow, a change of control or of province is not leaving it), and their stack removed. Accepted in any place, from any player, on any card.
- **Status tokens** (glossary, "Status Token"): a character in play or a province holds one at most of each kind (`CardState::personalHonor`, read by `ConflictService::assertCanHoldStatusToken()`). `PlaceStatusToken` (`token`: `Enum/StatusToken`, honored or dishonored) places one on a character in play or a province, any kind on either (the engine does not check that only a dishonored token goes on a province); `DiscardStatusToken` discards the token of a character in play or a province; `MoveStatusToken` moves the token of a character to another. A card receiving a token takes its status, or returns to ordinary status when it had the other token, both being discarded; a card cannot receive a second token of a kind. The cards become ordinary or take the status without being honored nor dishonored (`StatusTokenPlacedEvent`, `StatusTokenDiscardedEvent`, `StatusTokenMovedEvent`, not `PersonalHonorChangedEvent`), so that what reacts to honoring or dishonoring is not triggered; accepted in any place, from any player, on any player's cards. Tainted status tokens are not modeled.
- **Honor tokens** (counters, not status tokens: Fortified Assembly, Public Forum, Distinguished Dojo, Endless Archives): `PlaceHonorTokens` and `RemoveHonorTokens` (`card_id`, `amount`) place them from the token pool on a province or a holding face up in a province (`ConflictService::assertCanHoldHonorTokens()`), or return them there; the honor of the players does not change (`CardState::honorTokens`, `HonorTokensPlacedEvent`, `HonorTokensRemovedEvent`). A holding leaving play (leaving its province face up: discarded, sacrificed…) returns its tokens to the pool (`Workflow/DynastyCard/ProvinceFaceupLeaveListener`); switched to another province, it keeps them. Accepted in any place, from any player, on any player's cards.
- **Pointing at a card** (`PointAtCard`): changes nothing but the log ("{player} points at {card} in {stack}"), so that a player shows a card to the opponent; accepted in any place, from any player, on a card of a stack every player sees (not a deck nor a hand), face down included (its code stays masked).
- **Totals from players**: the engine does not compute skills, province strengths or glory, which card effects modify: players declare them (`DeclareConflictSkill`, `DeclareProvinceStrength`, `DeclareGloryCount`) and the engine applies the rules to the declared values (winner, broken provinces, Imperial Favor). The current skill of a character can also be recorded for every player to see (`SetCharacterSkill` / `ResetCharacterSkill`, every modifier applied, cleared when it leaves play); it is not used by the engine.
