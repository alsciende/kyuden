# Card scripts

What the text of a card means for a game in progress is its script: a subclass of `AbstractCardScript` named after its code (`KakitaKaezin01` for `01-kakita-kaezin`, in `src/CardScript`, which holds `AbstractCardScript` and the scripts only, no pack nor cycle in the namespace), created by `make:app:card-script <code> [--timing=<timing>…] [--granted-to-attached] [--special-action=<type>…]` (`Maker/MakeCardScript`). A card whose text needs nothing has no script. `AbstractCardScript` declares the code (`getCode()`, static) and a method per hook, with a default doing nothing that a card overrides: what is printed on the card, `getAbilityTimings()` (the timings of its triggered abilities, those it grants included: `Enum/AbilityTiming`, action, interrupt, reaction) and `isAbilityGrantedToAttached()` (the attachments whose abilities belong to the card they are attached to, "Attached character gains…"), which `CardScriptRegistry` reads by code for the events played (`PlayService`), the controller of an ability (`AbilityService`) and `GET /api/cards` (none for a card without script); and, given the physical card and the game, `getSpecialActions()`, the special messages its text needs (as classes). The scripts are tagged services found by code (`Service/CardScript/CardScriptRegistry`, a service locator indexed by `getCode()`), stateless and shared by every game: the state stays in the game, so that replaying its action records gives the same game, and nothing is registered per game (the worker is a long-running process: what a game would register would stay for the next ones). The services using the scripts are apart, in `Service/CardScript`: `ActiveCardsResolver` gives the active cards of a player, those whose effects they can apply, with their scripts (`Model/ActiveCard`): the cards they control in play and the cards they own out of play, among those whose face they know (`Visibility`: not their decks nor their facedown dynasty cards), so that what the scripts tell them reveals nothing they do not know. A script informs the players (the actions offered…), it never decides: the engine does not enforce the card rules. A test checks that every class of `src/CardScript` is a script named after the code of a card of the fixtures, and only needs special messages.

To add the script of a card:

1. Read its text (`GET /api/cards/{code}`, or `fixtures/`) and its rulings (`doc/card-clarifications.md`): the timings of its triggered abilities (action, interrupt, reaction: its events are played with the message of their timing and need no script), whether it grants them to the card it is attached to ("Attached character gains…"), and the special messages its effects need (the types marked `special`, `GET /api/actions`). A card whose text needs none of them has no script.
2. When an effect needs a message that does not exist yet, write it first (`MakeAction`, `#[AppAction(special: true)]` when few cards need it), then `app:message-schemas:dump` and `make front-messages`, and offer it in the web client (a mockup first): the card waits in "Cards not playable yet" of `HANDOFF.md` meanwhile.
3. Generate the script: `bin/console make:app:card-script <code> [--timing=action|interrupt|reaction…] [--granted-to-attached] [--special-action=<type>…]` (in `make sh`), which checks the code, the timings and the types, and names the class after the code.
4. Override what else its text needs (`getAbilityStacksOutOfPlay()`: the stacks out of play where its abilities are used, "put this character into play from your hand"); `getSpecialActions()` may read the card and the game (stateless: the state stays in the game). The structural tests (`CardScriptRegistryTest`) check the name and the special messages; a script that reads the card or the game gets its own test, in `tests/CardScript`.
5. Nothing to do in the web client nor the house bot: `GET /api/cards` gives the timings (`ability_timings`, `ability_granted_to_attached`, `ability_stacks_out_of_play`), the view of the game the special actions (`special_actions`). Take the card out of "Cards not playable yet" when it was there.

**Reaction offers.** A card reacting to a triggering condition says so statically: `getSubscribedEvents()` maps the class of a domain event implementing `Event/TriggeringEventInterface` (a name, `getTriggerName()`, and the ids its clients need, `getTriggerParams()`) to a public method of the script, `on<Event>(<Event> $event, PhysicalCard $card, Game $game): list<Model/ReactionOffer>`, which decides whether that copy reacts (`Service/CardScript/TriggerConditions` holds the common conditions: controlled by a player, of an element, participating, attacking, the province attacked, a conflict in progress). `App\Kernel` compiles the subscriptions of every script (the method by code of card, by class of event: `.app.card_script_subscriptions`, checked at compile time) and registers `Service/CardScript/ReactionOfferer` for each of these events, after their other listeners. Once the event is dispatched, it asks, for each player, their active cards whose script subscribes to it and that they can use where they are (an event in their hand; the abilities of a card where the engine accepts that they trigger them, `AbilityService::canTrigger()`: in play, or out of play where its script says, `getAbilityStacksOutOfPlay()`); the players offered a card each get a reaction window (`State/Model/ReactionWindow`: an id from `GameState::reactionWindowCount`, never from the generator, the trigger, its params, the ids of the cards), in `PlayerState`. The window offers, it blocks nothing (see `game-flow.md`). The forced reactions are offered marked forced (`ReactionOffer::$forced`): their effect is not the choice of their player, who cannot decline them, and the web client moves the game on for nobody until they are triggered. The triggering conditions done (all of them, their order and what each needs: `reactions.md`), each with a test listing its cards:

- a province revealed (`ProvinceRevealedEvent`, dispatched once the transition `reveal` of `province_card` has completed): 34 cards;
- a conflict won or lost (`ConflictWonEvent`, when the result is determined, in `react_to_conflict_result`; a conflict nobody wins triggers nothing): 88 cards.
- a conflict declared: once the attacked province is revealed, entering `react_to_conflict_declaration` (`ConflictDeclarationCompletedEvent`, after the transition `attacked_province_revealed`: the rules put the reactions there, and a province face down could not trigger its own), 25 cards; the declaration itself (`ConflictDeclaredEvent`, before the province is revealed: The Crashing Wave, and Enlightened Warrior, to the fate of the ring chosen, which the attacker takes at once), 2 cards.
- a ring claimed (`RingClaimedEvent`: by the winner of a conflict, in `react_to_ring_claim`, or by an effect, `ClaimRing`): 7 cards.
- a pass on declaring a conflict (`ConflictPassedEvent`): 9 cards.
- a province broken (`ProvinceBrokenEvent`: by the attacking player at the resolution of a conflict, before `react_to_broken_province`, or by the sender of `BreakProvince`): 9 cards.
- the honor dials revealed (`HonorDialsRevealedEvent`, once every dial of the bid is revealed: the draw phase, or a duel, its players only): 9 cards, those of a duel included.
- a character declared as an attacker (the declaration complete, above) or as a defender (`DefendersDeclaredEvent`): 2 cards.
- a phase begun (`PhaseBeganEvent`, once a transition `*_phase_began` has completed, with the phase): 21 cards.
- a character or an attachment entered play (`CardEnteredPlayEvent`: played, by the handlers of `PlayCharacter`, `PlayCharacterFromProvince`, `PlayAttachment`, `PlayCharacterAsAttachment`, or put into play, `PutCharacterIntoPlay`, `PutAttachmentIntoPlay`, once it has joined the conflict, with the stack it came from): 22 cards.
- a card played (`CardPlayedEvent`: an event once in the discard pile, a character or an attachment after `CardEnteredPlayEvent`; playing is not putting into play), with the stack it came from and the fate spent: 35 cards; the targets of an event are not known (Withstand the Darkness, Dragon Tattoo: offered for every event), nor the end of its effects (The Mirror's Gaze: offered once played).
- a duel won (`DuelWonEvent`, once both statistics are declared, the duel still in the state) and a duel resolved (`DuelEndedEvent`, with the characters involved, the duel no longer in the state): 5 cards.
- a character moved to the conflict (`CharacterMovedToConflictEvent`, `MoveToConflict`): 7 cards; "commits to a conflict" (Spyglass, Daikyū) is being declared as an attacker or a defender (the events above), "played into a conflict" (Hida Tsuru) a character played participating (`CardPlayedEvent`).
- fate placed on or removed from a character (`FatePlacedOnCardEvent`, with whether by a card effect, `PlaceFate`; `FateRemovedFromCardEvent`, with whether it leaves play, which is not removing fate; `FateMovedEvent`, moved to or from it), and removed in the fate phase as a whole (`FatePhaseFateRemovedEvent`, once after every character): 6 cards.
- the honor of a player (`HonorGainedEvent`, `HonorLostEvent`, with whether by a card effect, `GainHonor` and `LoseHonor`, or by the rules; `HonorTransferredEvent`, with whether by an honor bid: honor given is neither lost nor gained, glossary "Honor"): 5 cards; whose effect makes a player lose honor is not known (Righteous Samurai: offered for every card effect).
- a character honored or dishonored (`PersonalHonorChangedEvent`, with the action, `HonorCharacter` or `DishonorCharacter`: a dishonored character honored is ordinary and has been honored; none when it leaves play), or receiving a status token (`StatusTokenPlacedEvent`, not honoring): 4 cards; whose effect dishonors is not known (Ardent Omoidasu: offered for every character dishonored).
- the hand: cards discarded from it (`CardsDiscardedFromHandEvent`, once per message, `DiscardCard` from a hand and `DiscardRandomCardFromHand`), looked at or revealed (`CardsLookedAtEvent`, `CardsRevealedEvent`, of any stack: the scripts check it is a hand): 4 cards; whose effect discards is not known (Akodo Motivator: offered for every discard from the hand).
- the dynasty phase: a pass (`DynastyPhasePassedEvent`, with the fate gained for passing first) and the fate collected (`FateCollectedEvent`): 4 cards, Sneaky Shinjo and Ride at Dawn (a dynasty event) from their provinces.
- fate gained from a ring (`RingFateTakenEvent`, the contested ring, once the conflict is in the state; `FateMovedEvent` from a ring to a player) and the effect of the fire ring (`PersonalHonorChangedEvent` in `resolve_ring_effects`, the winner of a conflict for the fire ring): 4 cards; Medium of the Living Soul grants its reaction for a conflict, an effect the engine does not keep.
- a card bowed or readied (`CardBowedEvent`, `CardReadiedEvent`, after the transitions, with whether by a card effect, `BowCard` and `ReadyCard`, or by the rules: the participants of a conflict bowed as they return home, the conflict still in the state; the cards readied in the fate phase): 3 cards; whose effect bows is not known (Ready for Battle: offered for every character bowed by an effect).
- a dynasty card turned face up in its province (`DynastyCardTurnedFaceupEvent`, once the transition `turn_faceup` of `dynasty_card` has completed): 2 cards, Lost Papers (a dynasty event) during the dynasty phase only.
- a card left play (`CardLeftPlayEvent`, at the end of `LeavePlayListener`, with its controller and whether it was a character): 2 cards. `TriggerConditions::isPhase()` reads the phase of a place (`Enum/Phase::ofPlace()`).
- an ability triggered (`AbilityTriggeredEvent`, by `AbilityService::trigger()`, with the character triggering it: the card in play, or the character an attachment grants it to): 2 cards.
- the end of the resolution of a conflict (`ConflictResolvedEvent`, once its participants have returned home, with the characters that were attacking and the provinces it broke): 2 cards.
